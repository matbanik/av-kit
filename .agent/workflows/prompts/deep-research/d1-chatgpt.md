# D1: ChatGPT — Breadth & Quantitative Research

> **Platform**: ChatGPT (Deep Research mode)
> **Strength exploited**: Multi-source web crawling, quantitative data aggregation, real URL/price discovery

## Prompt Template

```
Research {TOPIC} comprehensively across multiple sources.

PART 1 — CURRENT STATE & LANDSCAPE
- What are the current solutions, tools, and approaches for {TOPIC}?
- For each: name, description, last update date, community size, GitHub stars (if applicable)
- Compare alternatives in a table format

PART 2 — TECHNICAL DEEP DIVE
- How does {TOPIC} work technically? Architecture, dependencies, limitations
- What are the known issues, bugs, or limitations?
- What are the performance benchmarks or metrics?

PART 3 — COMMUNITY & ECOSYSTEM
- What are users saying on Reddit, GitHub Issues, Stack Overflow, HN?
- What are the most common complaints and praised features?
- Quote specific user comments with source URLs

PART 4 — BEST PRACTICES
- What are the recommended approaches for production use?
- What pitfalls should be avoided?
- What configuration or tuning is commonly recommended?

CITATION RULES: Tag every factual claim with its source URL inline. End with a ## Sources section listing all URLs used.
```
