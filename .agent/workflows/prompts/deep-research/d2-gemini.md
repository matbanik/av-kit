# D2: Gemini — Trends & Search Intent

> **Platform**: Gemini (Deep Research mode)
> **Strength exploited**: Google Search grounding, People Also Ask data, autocomplete patterns, real-time search index

## Prompt Template

```
Analyze trends, search patterns, and community signals for {TOPIC}.

PART 1 — SEARCH INTENT ANALYSIS
- What are people searching for related to {TOPIC}?
- What are the top Google autocomplete suggestions?
- What appears in "People Also Ask" for key queries?
- What search volume trends exist (rising, stable, declining)?

PART 2 — TREND VELOCITY
- Is {TOPIC} gaining or losing traction?
- What new developments or releases are driving interest?
- What are the emerging sub-topics or related technologies?
- Timeline: key events in the last 12 months

PART 3 — COMMUNITY SIGNALS
- What subreddits, forums, or Discord servers discuss {TOPIC}?
- What are the trending discussions in these communities?
- What questions keep recurring that lack good answers?

PART 4 — OPPORTUNITY GAPS
- What aspects of {TOPIC} are underserved by existing content?
- What documentation or guides are missing?
- What tools or integrations do users want but don't exist?

CITATION RULES: Tag every factual claim with its source URL inline. End with a ## Sources section listing all URLs used.
```
