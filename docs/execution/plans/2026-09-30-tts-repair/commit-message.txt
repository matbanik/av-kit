feat(tts): provision runtime assets, surface failures, rework model and text panels

MEU-62 makes TTS produce audio again. Managed downloads now provide the
ONNX vocab and a pinned, sha256-verified en_core_web_sm wheel, and both
appear as Settings readiness rows. A missing asset fails fast with a 409
that names it. Every Failed job carries a reason that reaches the Console
and the run bar. Real-network checks found two more defects, now fixed:
Kokoro output past +/-1.0 is clipped, and kokoro 0.9.4 KPipeline.Result
chunks are unpacked.

MEU-63 gives the model picker a single action per variant, an inline
details panel and asset chips. MEU-64 adds Cleanup Apply, removes the raw
preview and adds a voice combobox with a language filter. Speed and
Format now stay above the fold.

Fixes found by the Windows gate:
- the main window is revealed on ready-to-show, and boot survives an
  aborted first load;
- the Settings rail click lock lasts until the smooth scroll settles;
- tests restore sys.path after each test.

on 407db19. TTS_LIVE passed 5/5 and pytest 629/0, Vitest 702/702. e2e had
117 passed and 1 unexpected, which Mat attributed to his own input and
