### Evidence-First & Sequencing Rules

Evidence records follow `.agent/docs/output-evidence-policy.md`. Raw receipts are working data. Each completed row points to durable pasted observations in `walkthrough.md` and the handoffs.

- **Evidence-first completion:** a row is `[x]` only when its deliverable exists and the handoff or walkthrough carries the evidence bundle (AGENTS.md §Execution Contract).
- **The review barrier resolves the old reflection↔review cycle:** the handoffs (H1-6) are created *before* the review, because they are its input. The reflection and metrics (H2) are created *after* `approved`, so they can record the verdict. Do not create H2 artifacts during H1, and do not dispatch the review (H1-7) before the handoffs exist.
- **Self-review prohibition:** the implementing agent dispatches and collects the H1-7 review. It never authors the `approved` verdict itself (AGENTS.md §Execution Contract).
- **`[B]` is evidence-gated:** valid ONLY for (a) a reproduced *external* error, (b) a missing dependency, credential or permission, or (c) a human-decision gate.
  - Each needs a linked follow-up. For (a) and (b), it also needs a row-bound `### B-<row-id>` block holding the command, the nonzero exit and the pasted decisive error as `evidence.v1`. A decision needs a durable decision link.
  - Subjective reasons ("too complex", "no time", "out of scope", "later") are invalid. No error artifact means not blocked, so keep working (AGENTS.md §Execution Contract).
- **Dispatching the H1-7 review is a blocking step, not a turn boundary:** continue to H2 in the same pass once the `approved` verdict file lands. The only sanctioned turn-enders are DONE, the review cap, reviewer unavailable, or a human decision. Compaction continues (AGENTS.md §Execution Contract).
- **Stop conditions S1–S7** (plan §Stop Conditions) are the human-decision gates of this plan. Hitting one is a `[B]` (c) with a Decision Brief.
